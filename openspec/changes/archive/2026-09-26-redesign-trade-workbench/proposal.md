## Why

The selected trade workflow still uses an undifferentiated field grid and distant
general instructions. History actions reveal an editor above the viewport while
keyboard focus remains in the history. Shared results mix styled and unstyled actions.

## What Changes

- Group trade identity/direction, exact amounts and execution time; associate concise
  Russian guidance with its fields while retaining all labels and precise string input.
- Explicit history correction/void actions reveal, focus and scroll to the editor;
  cancellation returns to the originating action when still available.
- Make shared result actions, captions and spacing consistent with the theme and
  accessible control system; retain semantic tables, complete evidence and exact totals.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `trade-workbench`: Accessible grouped trade entry and deliberate history-to-editor focus.

### Modified Capabilities
None. Existing financial, workflow, context and recovery contracts remain unchanged.

## Impact

TradeForm, TradeJournal composition/focus and locally scoped presentation. Extend
existing critical browser journeys; preserve characterization for presentation-only
results. Depends on0370395 and archived account/workflow/context slices. No backend,
schema, dependency, deployment or data changes. Other editors/screens, chart periods,
validation redesign and private preview refresh remain separate work.
